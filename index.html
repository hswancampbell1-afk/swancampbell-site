The guarantee

Nothing goes out in your name without you approving it.

Replies are drafted, never sent. Calendar invitations go only when you press the button. That isn't a promise about how I work — it's built into the software I use, and an automated test fails if anyone ever changes it.

Drafts appear in your own Drafts folder as mail arrives, so you may see one before I have. I review every one within one business day; my review improves them, and you decide what gets sent.

On Microsoft 365 it goes further: the permission you grant me doesn't include sending at all. It's on Microsoft's own consent screen before you agree, and in your admin panel afterwards. Google's nearest equivalent is broader and does allow it, so there the guarantee rests on the software alone. I'd rather tell you which is which than let you assume they're the same.
